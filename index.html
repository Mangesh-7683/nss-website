Introduction to NSS

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a youth program aimed at developing students’ personality through community service. It promotes values of social responsibility, empathy, and leadership. NSS volunteers actively engage in social, cultural, and environmental activities, fostering unity, discipline, and a spirit of service toward nation-building.
